Mohsin Mahmud Topu

I am an aspiring transportation researcher and a recent graduate of Civil and Environmental Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology.

My research is dedicated to developing safe, reliable, robust, efficient, and capable machine learning systems for intelligent and sustainable transportation. Over the past few years, my work has moved toward a single goal: building human-centric intelligent transportation systems that strengthen mobility and infrastructure (pavement management, mobility behaviour, and sustainability). To reach this goal, I am developing myself along three interconnected directions:

  • [1] Intelligent & Sustainable Infrastructure, to design data-driven models and explainable intelligence for infrastructure condition assessment, predictive maintenance, and uncertainty-aware decision-making.
  • [2] Mobility Behaviour & Urban Transport Planning, to develop data-driven understanding of travel choices and their implications for urban mobility, transportation demand, and energy-efficient systems.
  • [3] Human-Centric AI for Intelligent Transportation, to ensure measurable safety, reliability, interpretability, and human alignment in intelligent transportation systems.

My overarching aim is to turn intelligent transportation into smarter, more sustainable, and energy-aware urban mobility for future cities and communities.
